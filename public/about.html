About KarmaMap

AI Fortune × Career Strategy — Free Forever

What is KarmaMap?

KarmaMap is a free web application that combines five ancient fortune-telling traditions with modern AI to give you a clear, practical view of your career trajectory. Instead of vague predictions, KarmaMap delivers a 2026 career score, your top 5 peak years, quarterly attack/defend timing, and a concrete 30/60/90-day action plan.

We believe the world's oldest wisdom systems — when analyzed together — reveal patterns that no single system can see alone. KarmaMap is the cross-analysis layer that makes this accessible to everyone, for free.

The 5 Systems

🀄 사주팔자 (Four Pillars) Korean/Chinese destiny system based on year, month, day, and hour of birth.
🔢 Numerology Western and Pythagorean number patterns derived from birth date and name.
⭐ Western Astrology Natal chart analysis — sun sign, rising, planetary placements, 2026 transits.
🕉️ Vedic Jyotish Indian Vedic astrology with Dasha cycles and sidereal placements.
☯️ 紫微斗數 (Zi Wei Dou Shu) Chinese imperial astrology — palace system based on birth time and date.

How It Works

Step 1 — Your Profile: Enter your date of birth, birth city, and optionally your birth time for the most accurate reading.

Step 2 — Career Context: Share your current role, years of experience, and your primary goal (promotion, career change, startup, income growth, or leadership).

Step 3 — Choose Your Systems: Select which of the 5 fortune systems to include in your cross-analysis.

Result: Google Gemini AI cross-analyzes all selected systems and generates your personalized report — including your 2026 score, peak years, quarterly windows, and an AI chat for follow-up questions.

Who Is It For?

  • Professionals in Singapore and Southeast Asia navigating major career decisions in 2026.
  • Anyone curious about how ancient wisdom systems describe their personal timing and energy.
  • People who want an AI-powered second opinion before making a big move — job change, startup launch, negotiation, or promotion push.

Is It Free?

Yes — KarmaMap is 100% free. The service is supported by Google AdSense advertising. No payment, account creation, or subscription is required to receive your full reading.

Who Made This?

KarmaMap is a personal project built and operated by an individual based in Singapore. The project is powered by the Google Gemini API, Firebase, and React.
