Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2025  ·  Last Updated: April 20, 2025

KarmaMap ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal project operated by an individual based in Singapore. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use karmamap-dd922.web.app (the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To generate your AI-powered fortune and career analysis, you voluntarily provide:

  • Date of birth
  • Birth time (optional)
  • Birth city
  • Current job or industry
  • Years of experience
  • Career goal (e.g., job change, promotion, startup)
  • Selected fortune analysis systems

We also collect:

  • Usage data – pages visited, interactions, browser type, device type, and IP address (processed by Google Firebase and Google Analytics).
  • Comments (optional) – if you submit a comment on a result page, we store your display name, auto-generated result summary, optional comment text, language preference, and a hashed version of your IP address for spam prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your personalized fortune and career report via the Google Gemini API.
  • To display community comments on the result page.
  • To prevent spam (hashed IP stored in Firestore for rate-limiting).
  • To improve the Service and understand usage patterns.
  • To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdSense
We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Google Gemini API
Your birth details and career information are sent to the Google Gemini API to generate your fortune report. This data is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Firebase / Firestore
We use Firebase (Google Cloud) to host the application and store community comments. Data is stored on Google's servers. See Firebase Privacy Information.

Nominatim / OpenStreetMap
City search autocomplete uses the Nominatim geocoding API, which may log your search query. No personal data is transmitted beyond the city name string.

4. Cookies

We and our third-party partners (primarily Google AdSense) use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Remember your language preference.
  • Serve and measure advertising performance.
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns.

Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Service.

5. Data Retention

  • Fortune reports are generated in real-time and not stored on our servers.
  • Community comments are stored indefinitely unless you request deletion.
  • IP rate-limit records are automatically overwritten after one hour.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. International Users

The Service is operated from Singapore. If you access the Service from outside Singapore, your information may be transferred to and processed in Singapore or on Google's global infrastructure.
